About me

A bit about me

I hold a Master's degree in Physics Engineering from the University of Porto, which gave me a strong foundation in problem-solving and analytical thinking before I moved into software.

I currently work as a Software Developer at Wish and Cook, a startup building automated cooking stations in partnership with the University of Aveiro. I work across the full stack — React and Tailwind on the frontend, Nest.js on the backend (previously Django) — building the platform end to end. I'm also involved in an AI research scholarship at the university on the side.
