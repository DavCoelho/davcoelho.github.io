About me

A bit about me

I hold a Master's degree in Physics Engineering from the University of Porto, which gave me a strong foundation in problem-solving and analytical thinking. The transition into software was a natural progression — the same rigour that physics demands translates directly into how I approach code.

I currently work as a Software Developer at Wish and Cook, a startup building automated cooking stations in partnership with the University of Aveiro. I work across the full stack — React and Tailwind on the frontend, Nest.js on the backend (previously Django) — and have hands-on experience integrating AI capabilities, including the OpenAI API, into production systems.

I care about writing code that's easy to read and easy to follow — where the logic speaks for itself. I like to take my time with a problem, understand it properly, and get it right. Working across the full stack in a small team has made me comfortable picking up whatever a feature needs, from the API to the interface.
